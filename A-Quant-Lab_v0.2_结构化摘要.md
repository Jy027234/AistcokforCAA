## 1. Scope & non-goals

**What the product is**
- Core positioning (line 8): 每天用同一套可追溯的数据和研究规则观察市场，综合行业、公司、宏观、新闻与行为信号，形成可验证的研究假设；由用户决定是否调整模拟组合，再检验模型和人的判断分别增加了什么价值。
- §1.3 line 63: **不是"让 AI 每天替我挑几只股票"，而是"让证据、规则和我的选择都可以被验证"**。Users answer 4 questions daily (lines 65-70): what really changed today; what is worth researching and what the evidence/counter-evidence is; what difference holding / following the model / self-adjusting makes; what happened later to earlier judgements and whether the error came from data, interpretation, selection or execution.
- Usage (§0.1 line 32): 单人、研究和模拟、中文 Web 界面、支持手机浏览. Research frequency: 日频刷新，初始策略每周调仓; no intraday high-frequency, no real-time order book, no second-level news front-running.
- Delivery nature (line 6, line 26): development spec; commands/interfaces/directories are targets, **not evidence that a runnable product exists**.

**Explicit non-goals / refusals**
- Line 10: 首期不连接券商、不自动交易、不对外提供付费荐股或代客理财; all capital/positions/thresholds/strategy weights are software research initial parameters, not investment advice, not expected return.
- §3.3 line 137 (verbatim): 不做实盘接口、融资融券、期货期权、日内高频、Level-2 撮合、自动搜索无限因子、自动强化学习调仓、开放用户插件、公开荐股社区、多租户计费、知识图谱数据库和复杂微服务体系。
- §17.1 line 816: 首期定位为自用研究与模拟，不提供真实交易通道，也不承诺收益. Live trading is a **separate project** (§3.4 line 147) that must re-verify data rights, regulation, broker interfaces, account suitability and risk control; "是否实盘不是本项目自然自动发生的下一步".
- §17.4 line 836: phase 1 runs no arbitrary Python generated by users or the model (later: separate sandbox, no network, CPU/memory/time limits, read-only inputs, review before becoming a strategy version).
- §18.4 line 886: 不以收益率作为软件验收条件. Also no "综合投资价值 87.36 分" style score (§5.3 line 226), no one-click real buy (§5.3 line 222), no automatic meta-strategy weights (ADR 008), no RL / auto news-score trading / multi-agent debate order placement in phase 1 (§10.6 line 496).

**Scope split (§3.1, lines 114-120)**
- Observation scope: scan A-shares according to obtainable data; which exchanges/boards are covered must be shown explicitly on the data-capability page — "不能只取部分列表，却显示全市场已覆盖".
- Default simulatable scope: 沪深交易所普通主板 A股, excluding (as known at decision time) risk-warning, delisting-consolidation, newly listed fewer than the required trading days, suspended, key fields missing, liquidity-insufficient. 创业板、科创板、北交所首期可展示，但默认不进入可执行模拟池. Future expansion must add trading rules, corporate actions and tests board by board — "不允许靠修改一个过滤条件完成".
- §3.2 (lines 122-134) phase-1 must-deliver, 8 rows: 数据与快照 / 今日工作台 / 研究卡片 / 基线策略 / 模拟账户 / 实验与复盘 / AI助手 / 运行保障 (idempotent jobs, data-failure degradation, rerun records, backup+restore, fee and model-call usage).

## 3. Data model

**Important caveat:** the main doc gives **no column-level DDL** for these objects. §15.1 line 747 says the bundle SQL (`schema/001_metadata.sql`) is only 元数据和账本基础结构; 行情/财务长表、完整公司行为明细及历史行业成员 are to be filled in by migrations and Parquet contracts, and "基础 SQL 通过语法验证不代表已经实现领域不变量". Likewise §16.2 line 796 says ports/routes/OpenAPI are generated during development.

### 3.1 Storage split (§15.1, lines 734-747)
- 数值事实放 Parquet，事务性对象和引用索引放 SQLite. Normalized rows must carry: 证券/指标 ID, 日期, 供应商和版本, 单位, 可用时点或其关联索引. Every physical file is listed in an immutable manifest.
- Six domains, 主要对象 / 关键关联 (verbatim, lines 738-745):
  - 数据: 来源、数据版本、快照、快照成员、证券及状态版本 — 每次研究明确指向输入快照
  - 证据: 原始材料、引用片段、事件、事件-引用关联 — 所有事实声明回到原材料
  - 研究: 研究运行、策略版本、实验和实验组 — 固定代码、配置、特征与数据版本
  - 决策: 用户决定、模拟草稿、冻结计划 — 保存模型原方案和人工差异
  - 账户: 模拟组合、订单、成交、持仓批次、现金/应收账本、估值 — 从期初到期末可以逐项对账
  - 运维: 任务、模型调用、审计日志 — 重跑、权限、额度与错误可诊断

### 3.3 Daily schedule / snapshot lifecycle (§8.1, lines 350-362)
- 交易日收盘后: update quotes, status and corporate actions and keep collecting licensed material → per-domain validation results.
- 20:30 Asia/Shanghai: daily research input cutoff, then compute and publish the EOD snapshot (basis for the today workbench and next-day drafts).
- Next trading day 08:45: collect overnight material and form the pre-open final input snapshot (the decision basis shared by all comparison accounts).
- 08:55: freeze the eligible model and user simulated plans for that trading day → immutable decision and order list.
- After the open / after EOD data is complete: settle simulated open fills and EOD valuation.
- Times are configurable but changes must be versioned. The 20:30 cutoff does not guarantee vendors deliver everything by then; incomplete data shows waiting or blocks. If the 08:45 key snapshot is late, 08:55 produces no new orders and never silently falls back to an old snapshot. Weekly rebalance runs on the first trading day of the week using price signals from the previous trading day; no-trade weeks are skipped naturally.

## 6. Ledger & simulation rules (§12, plus §8 and §11)

- **Correctness boundary (§12.1):** simulated fills are a computation under an explicit convention, not a promise of real broker fills. Phase 1 supports only validated ordinary main-board stocks, cash long-only, daily frequency, all-or-nothing open-price simulated orders. No order-book queueing, no intraday stop-loss/take-profit, no fine-grained partial fills. Adopting a limited simulator is for complexity control, but that does not make the constraints optional: unsupported trades or corporate actions must raise an explicit error and never fall into an approximation that reports success.
- **Rules are not permanent constants (§12.2):** the trading-rule table is matched by exchange + board + security status + effective date. SSE published a trading-rule revision on 2026-04-24 with main-board risk-warning price-limit changes effective 2026-07-06 [S16]; do not hard-code "ST always 5%", "all A-shares always 100-share lots" or one global fee. Even though phase 1 excludes ST, status, rules and each day price limits must still be retained historically for observation, exclusion and existing positions. Trading-quantity rules are accepted board by board.
- **Timing & price (§12.3):** pre-open frozen orders are simulated at that day open at the earliest; never decide with the close and fill at the same close; never pretend the optimal share count was decided before seeing the open. Target shares use the reference price knowable at decision time and reserved cash. Buy = open + pre-registered slippage, sell = open − slippage; if the intended price crosses the day legal price boundary it is **not executable** (never clamp to the boundary to manufacture a fill). Buy blocked by an open limit-up, sell by an open limit-down → default no fill. Suspension, no valid open price, or unknown key status → no fill. Reports must state that queueing and open liquidity are not simulated.
- **Volume & cash (§12.4):** open order size may only be capped by previously known volume (never the same-day close volume, which may only be used to review capacity risk afterwards). Simulation order: process sell orders first in a stable order, then buy orders in a stable order. If a sell does not fill, its expected proceeds do not exist. If fully filled buys plus fees exceed available cash, the whole order is rejected — no overdraft and no smart shrinking with future data. The default assumes same-day sell proceeds are usable for buys (a simplification); a **conservative control mode in which buys use only pre-open cash must be provided** and the difference between the two assumptions must be reported.
- **T+1 and lots (§12.5):** each buy lot keeps its buy trading day and earliest sellable day; sell quantity may not exceed that day sellable lots; same-day new lots are not sellable. Normal buys follow integer lots per the supported market rules; odd-lot selling is implemented separately per validated rules; **no floating-point share counts**. Tests must distinguish "held the same stock yesterday" from "bought the same stock today" — an old position must not make new shares same-day sellable.
- **Fees & taxes (§12.6):** commission, minimum commission, sell stamp duty, transfer fee and similar use independent, date-effective fee versions. The official finance/tax announcement halves securities-transaction stamp duty from 2023-08-28; actual applicable rates, billing scope and account fees must be verified against the rules and the actual charging agreement in force at implementation time [S17]. Do not reuse open-source sample rates and claim they are current real costs. The fee file in this bundle is a **synthetic test rate deliberately forbidden for formal research on real data**, and M0 must separately build a verified fee table. Fees must not miss the minimum amount and must not be charged twice because an order was viewed multiple times. Monetary arithmetic uses `Decimal` or integer minor currency units; prices may be stored as micro-yuan integers; ledger amounts are rounded to cents as specified; never rely on binary floating point to keep cash balanced.
- **Corporate actions (§12.7):** must handle supported cash-dividend entitlement confirmation, dividend receivable, payment date, bonus/rights shares and cost adjustment. Entitlement is fixed by the record time point; on the ex-date the receivable and the price impact are recognized; on the payment date the receivable turns into cash; the ex-date loss and the cash dividend must not be double counted. Tax treatment must be labelled: without complete dividend-tax handling, output an explicit pre-tax or conservative tax scenario and never call it an accurate personal after-tax return; comparison benchmarks use a consistent treatment. Rights issues, complex mergers/share exchanges, spin-offs or corporate actions that cannot be verified correctly → stop producing new valid performance conclusions and mark them pending; never delete that stock whole history afterwards to make the backtest look better. Delisted stocks keep their history and exit event with a justified terminal value or an explicit conservative scenario; never value them forever at the last close as if liquidatable.
- **EOD valuation and invariants (§12.8):** 每日净值 = 可用及冻结现金 + 支持的应收项目 + 持仓估值 − 应付项目. Suspended stocks are valued at the explicitly recorded last valid price with the staleness day count marked, and the portfolio illiquidity risk must still be reflected. Must hold: cash never overdrawn; positions never negative; total shares consistent with lots; filled quantity not greater than order quantity; fees booked exactly once; cash line items summing to the balance; snapshot/order/fill/valuation all traceable. **Test failures must block net-value publication — not merely print a warning.**
- **Idempotency & reconciliation:** job idempotency key = job type + trading day + config version + input snapshot with a DB unique constraint (§8.4); failed reruns must not rewrite orders, billing or publish the same report twice; §15.1 account domain requires item-by-item reconciliation from period start to end; `GET /portfolios/{id}/ledger` returns positions, lots, net value and reconciliation; §11.4 booked simulated fills are never physically deleted (reversal + new version + marked affected reports); §12.6 no duplicate fee on repeated order views; golden case S05 retries the same frozen plan and records orders/fees/fills exactly once.
- **Portfolio build order (§11.1, 7 steps):** 1) read frozen input, position lots and current status; 2) mark cannot-sell / cannot-buy / unresolved corporate action limits (existing risk does not disappear because of filtering); 3) form the research target set from strategy ranks and the retention buffer; 4) produce target weights within total position, single-security and industry caps, keeping cash when constraints are insufficient; 5) convert weight differences into integer-share orders using prices known at decision time; 6) check expected cash, fees, sellable quantity and the past-volume order-size cap; 7) generate a diff preview and freeze the plan, then re-check the day status and cash at simulated execution. Existing unsellable positions consume risk budget first (e.g. an industry already frozen at 25% with a 30% cap leaves only 5% for other stocks — you may not ignore it and then allocate 30%); natural cap breaches from price moves are displayed and block added exposure, and a breach discovered at EOD may never be back-derived into an open sell.
- **Rebalance buffer (§10.3):** existing still-eligible holdings inside the top 30 are preferentially retained; gaps are filled by rank; conflict-resolution rules between new, retained and untradable objects must be deterministic and never left to the AI to improvise; the no-buffer version is kept as a separate experiment and never swapped in after poor results.
- **Decision freeze fairness (§8.2):** model, event shadow and human portfolios use the same final snapshot, the same order cutoff and the same simulated fill rules; the human using external unarchived information is recorded with `external_information_used=true` and flagged as no longer a strict same-information comparison; in forward experiments the model may not use last night material while the human is compared using post-open news.

### 7.3 AI & assistant cases (§18.3 lines 870-877)
Prepare an independent human-annotated set, **suggested ≥200 deduplicated materials**, covering amount units, same-name companies, negative announcements, reposts, revisions, contradictions and irrelevant news (the size is an engineering starting point, not a claim of statistical sufficiency). ### 7.4 Release gates (§18.4 lines 878-889)
- All cash and PIT golden tests pass; end-to-end tests cover at least one complete decision → simulation → valuation → review cycle.

- All securities to be traded have complete key fields. Market coverage uses actual expected records as the denominator and is interpreted by status such as suspension; even if the aggregate exceeds the illustrative 99.5% target, a candidate with missing key fields is blocked.
- Demo data is never mixed into real results; while fees/rules are unverified, formal research status cannot be "pass".
- At least these drills completed: backup and restore, job crash restart, idempotent retry, insufficient permission, data-source failure.
- No return rate is a software acceptance condition. Without a model key, synthetic tests and the verified price baseline must still run; without real data licenses only clearly marked demos are allowed, never pseudo-real research conclusions.
- Upgrade gate for a strategy to become default (§13.6): the user decides explicitly with an approval record, and at least PIT and ledger correctness, explainable out-of-sample and forward records, stable evidence against the control, conclusions not overly fragile under cost stress, and no reliance on a few special samples. There is no "auto go-live above X% backtest return"; 20 consecutive stable trading days is an engineering observation threshold, not a sufficient profit-validation period.
